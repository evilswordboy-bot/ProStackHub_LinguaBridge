Privacy Policy for LinguaBridge AI

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

Developer: LinguaBridge AI Team / Sakthibalan

At LinguaBridge AI, accessible as a mobile and web application, we respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our services.

1. Information We Process

LinguaBridge AI is designed to minimize user data collection:

  • Translation Inputs: When you input text for translation, this text is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS to our backend server and processed by Google's Gemini API strictly to return the translated output.
  • Local Storage: Your translation history, starred favorites, and personalized settings (e.g. theme, speech speed) are stored locally on your device's browser/app storage. We do not maintain a remote database of your personal translation history.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to provide your name, phone number, email address, or social security details to use the basic features of LinguaBridge AI.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide high-quality, context-aware translations, we utilize the following third-party infrastructure:

  • Google Gemini API: Processes your translation requests. Google's handling of data through enterprise APIs adheres to standard Google Cloud data protection terms.
  • Web Speech API: Text-to-speech audio playback utilizes your device's built-in operating system speech synthesis engines. Audio is rendered locally on your device.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your translation history is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete individual translations or clear your entire translation history at any time through the in-app "Clear History" button.
  • You can purge cached responses at any time using the "Clear Cache" button under Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

LinguaBridge AI does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our application provides general language translation utilities suitable for general audiences.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. All communications between the app, backend, and AI APIs occur via encrypted TLS/HTTPS protocols. Server-side API keys and credentials are never distributed or exposed to client-side code.
